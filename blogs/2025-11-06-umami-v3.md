Umami v3 brings boards, sharing, multi-value filtering, white-labeling, performance metrics, session replays, and more. Previous reports are now separated into individual pages, the filter form supports adding and editing multiple filters at once, and saved segments let you reuse filter sets. Links and pixels each get their own stats pages, and a new dedicated admin page lets admins manage all users, websites, and teams.
