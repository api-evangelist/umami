See where visitors click and scroll on your pages with Umami's new heatmaps feature. Click heatmaps overlay your page to show where visitors click, with hotter colors for more activity, while scroll heatmaps reveal how far down the page visitors actually get. They are useful for evaluating page layouts, landing pages after campaigns, and how much long-form content people read.
